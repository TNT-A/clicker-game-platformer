This is an experimental project of mine intended to merge the clicker based gameplay of an incremental game with that of a roguelike platformer.
A suprising twist on incremental clickers, Incremelee is a combat clicker in which you must rack up clicks in order to use your abilities! Defeat enemies to earn gold, then use that gold to boost your power to new heights!

The game is UNFINISHED! It's buggy and unbalanced in it's current state, but it's playable! I hope you enjoy seeing the game develop over time :)

Made in Godot, originally for Hack Club Roulette and then expanded on for HCTG & Macondo!

INSTRUCTIONS:
WASD to Move

Left Click to Charge Ability Bar (Clicking at 100% will fire)

Hold Right Click to Float (No using abilities while floating

Scrollwheel/Shift/Numkeys to Change Ability

Click items in the shop to purchase them

Click and drag anything in the shop onto your ability panel to upgrade, replace, or add new abilities!
