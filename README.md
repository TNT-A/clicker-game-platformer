This is an experimental project of mine intended to merge the clicker based gameplay of an incremental game with that of a roguelike platformer.

The game is UNFINISHED! It's buggy and unbalanced in it's current state, but it's playable! I hope you enjoy seeing the game develop over time :)

Made in Godot, originally for Hack Club Roulette and then expanded on for HCTG!

INSTRUCTIONS:
WASD to Move

Click to Charge Ability Bar

Scrollwheel to Change Ability

Down to Drop Through Platforms (Or drop to new levels!)

Click items in the shop to purchase them

Click and drag anything in the shop onto your ability panel to upgrade, replace, or add new abilities!

Click the LV Button to upgrade your click strength
